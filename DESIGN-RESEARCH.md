# Research record

2026-10-24. Product context: original Halloween quantum repair shop, not a quantum-money or CHSH reskin. This focused pass responds to the entrant's explicit request for understandable probes, more story and purposeful animation. It does not reopen the concept or justify copying another game's art.

## Supplied references, actually inspected

### Quantum Game with Photons
The supplied repository describes its 2014–2016 version as unmaintained and points to a newer game. Do not treat the old source and the current site as the same release.[1] Inspected the legacy browser's first-level surface: a concrete detector goal, visible apparatus, play/step/reset controls and direct manipulation.[2]

Also followed the supplied current entry point into Quantum Flytrap's modern The Beginning level: its first overlay points at the laser on the board, focusing attention on one piece of apparatus.[7][8] This was a first-screen inspection, not a complete modern-campaign playthrough.

Transfer: make the scientific change and its consequence visible next to the player's action. For our tutorial, a signal travels through a known training cable, then a real Qiskit result appears at the output. Do not copy its photon puzzles, board, illustration style, level data or code. This research did not establish its current mobile accessibility or finish its full campaign.

### Superposition Arcade
Read the supplied archived source README and licence, inspected its original screenshot and ran the public Unity browser build through its menu into the first level. The first level exposes one available X gate, two visible output lanes and a concrete receiving hole; later complexity is not dumped on that opening. Its documented mechanic maps rows of pegs to gates and moving balls to simulated circuit behavior.[3][5]

Transfer: teach one operation through a manipulation and visible consequence before naming the next instrument. Our Bit/Phase/Linked progression uses a cable bench rather than a copied peg board. Do not copy its balls, gates-as-pegs puzzle, assets, source or sounds. This was a first-level inspection, not a complete playthrough or a novice study.

The organizer's own 2023 results list Superposition Arcade first and describe the historical criteria as quantum scope, playability, originality and completeness.[4] These are evidence about that event, not a confirmed 2026 Track 3 rubric or a promise our entry will outrank it.

### Scientific foundation
IBM's worked Qiskit lesson prepares a shared Bell pair, applies local Pauli encodings to one partner and reunites them for decoding.[6] The repair-game framing is our contribution; the protocol is established. The tutorial must show the retained reference, one cable traversal and joint decoder, not imply two ordinary hidden bits or a second device test.

## Chosen direction

A workshop revealed through woods/fog; a short encounter with its keeper; a resettable training cable before any unexplained instrument choice; measured output lamps and an honest repair preview. Ticket arrival/stamps make progress tactile. Subtle CRT motion and quiet opt-in procedural sound make the workshop feel inhabited. Reduced motion presents the same fully readable information without waiting. Whimsy comes from art and causality, not slogans or extra paragraphs.

The new implementation and verification contract is docs/SCIENCE-WALKTHROUGH-CONTRACT.md. Proposed improvements above are our design judgments, not evidence of measured learning gains. Genuine beginner observations remain missing.

## Earlier reference retained

The Qubit Factory was inspected in the earlier design pass for a strong sense of place and action before equations. It already covers superdense coding; no artwork, layout, circuit palette, sound or level was copied. Its prior-art record remains in docs/PRIOR-ART.md. All in-project art and procedural sound for this pass is newly authored, with system fonts and no downloaded game assets.
