# Demo recording script — science walkthrough build

The organizer announcement supplied by the entrant requires a1–3 minute video. Aim for a rehearsed recording around two minutes; trim rather than exceed three. This is a recording target, not an already recorded video. Record the actual app and returned results. Never claim hardware execution or beginner-study results that did not happen.

## 1. Arrive at the workshop

Show the woods parting, warm window and keeper. Open the shop and choose Learn the tools. Sound can be enabled from Menu if it does not compete with narration; it starts off.

“This is Ghost in the Cable: a quantum repair shop. Before touching a customer's one-use cable, the keeper lets me learn on spare ones.”

## 2. Learn by operating the instruments

Send the clean Bit test, then the known X cable. Show the actual0 and1 returns. Continue to the Bit test of Z and the Phase test of Z. Show the+/− relation and the H decoder making a difference readable. No need to read every caption aloud.

“A Bit probe sends a known zero and checks whether it was swapped. But a phase flip can leave that test unchanged. The Phase probe prepares a superposition and turns a relative-phase difference into a readable bit.”

The plus/minus states are coherent superpositions, not random mixtures. The monitor shows test results, not the brightness of Mara's lantern.

## 3. Show the reason to keep a reference

Run the four resettable Bit cables, then the four Linked cables. Keep both paths and the joint decoder visible. Point to the identical ordinary results and four different joint results. Use the actual returned numbers, not an edited animation pretending to be a measurement.

“Each spare is a separate experiment. A real job allows one test. With four possible faults, an ordinary probe leaves ambiguity. The Linked probe keeps one entangled partner here and sends only the other through the cable. Reading them together separates the four ideal faults.”

Do not imply that the kept qubit alone reveals a remote fault or that two qubits mean two uses of the cable. First decoded light means bit flip; second means phase flip, in c1c0 order.

## 4. Turn a reading into a repair

Use the known training Y reading to set the two correction switches and align the preview, then Take the real ticket. Test Mara's real cable with Bit. If the returned bit is1, flip bit; if0, leave the switch off. Apply repair. Show the actual confirmed result and stamp.

“Now the lights tell me which correction is needed. Practice hasn't spent Mara's test. Her real repair is checked only when I apply it; the preview is not another query.”

Do not blindly switch on a correction in every recording. The real fault is not scripted.

## 5. Optional short real Linked repair and proof

If the recording has room, choose the Entanglement chapter after the first repair. Show and accept the unfinished-story confirmation rather than implying it preserves that active route. Use Linked on the first four-fault ticket, apply only corrections indicated by its actual two-bit reading, and briefly open Inspect circuit. Otherwise show Mara's actual circuit and Menu → The science.

“The measurements come from real Qiskit circuit simulation, not a physical quantum processor. The independent evidence also includes ordinary probes and an unentangled two-qubit control. This is about one-query identification—not computational speed.”

Close on the repaired device/stamp, not a fabricated completed eight-ticket receipt. Mention the short chapters if time permits.
