# What is established, what this project contributes

## Established physics
Pauli channel discrimination, Bell-state measurement and superdense coding are established. We explicitly rely on them; no new algorithm or theorem is claimed. IBM's lesson includes a worked Qiskit implementation: https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/basics-of-quantum-information/entanglement-in-action/qiskit-implementation . The channel-discrimination discussion and50%/100% separation appear in section2.1.1 of https://arxiv.org/html/2605.31519v1 .

## Existing educational games
The Qubit Factory already teaches superdense coding among many other protocols: https://arxiv.org/html/2406.11995v1 and https://www.qubitfactory.io/ . Its original circuits/art/assets/levels were not copied. We transfer only the broad idea that players can learn by operating a consistent fictional system.

A research-stage quantum-money proposal was rejected because closely overlapping games already exist, including Legal Tender: https://github.com/kinnuworks/legal-tender . This is evidence that the search changed the design, not proof the chosen concept is unprecedented.

## Intended contribution
An original Halloween repair-shop decision loop: diagnose a fresh single-use device, choose ordinary probes for restricted fault families, spend scarce entangled references on four-way uncertainty, and inspect the real circuit after committing to an answer. The eight-job economy makes instrument selection meaningful rather than a single button that always succeeds.

The game's artwork, text, UI implementation, tests and campaign are newly authored for this project, with AI assistance. Scientific credit remains with the cited prior work. We do not claim this is the first repair-themed quantum game or that a web search proves worldwide novelty.

## Asset ledger
No third-party images, audio, fonts, logos or copyrighted game assets were downloaded into the project. Original SVG/CSS illustrations; operating-system font stacks; any sound is synthesized locally after opt-in. Final asset audit still belongs in verification before release.
